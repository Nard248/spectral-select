# CODASSCA 2026 — A0 poster (paper 122, "Label-Free, Dependency-Aware Channel Selection for Coupled Multi-Channel Sensor Data")

**Deliverable:** `CODASSCA2026_poster_A0.pptx` — one A0 portrait slide (841 × 1189 mm), fully editable in PowerPoint.
`CODASSCA2026_poster_A0_preview.pdf` / `.png` are LibreOffice renders for a quick look (LibreOffice substitutes
Carlito for Calibri; the metrics are identical, so line breaks match PowerPoint).

The layout mirrors the IASIM 2026 Publisher poster: Arial Bold 64 pt title, Arial authors/affiliations with
superscripts, Calibri 22 pt body, Calibri Bold 40 pt section bars (khaki-gold `#E2D5A3` for the concept sections,
sage-green `#C4D0B5` for the data/results/footer sections), navy `#1A2332` table header with tinted rows, AUA and
L.A. Orbeli logos in the header, three-column top and bottom rows, full-width dataset bands in between.

  Edit in place, or edit the `CONTENT` dict at the top of `build_poster.py` and re-run
  `.venv/bin/python publications/codassca2026/poster/build_poster.py` (from the repo root) to rebuild.
* **Figures**: PNGs in `figures/`, drawn at their final poster size (fonts are true point sizes on the sheet),

## Numbers and provenance
All headline numbers are the paper's (Table I, Fig. 2, Sec. V-C). Extra numbers used only on the poster:
* EEG per-electrode selection frequencies, power and relevance maps: re-run of the paper protocol (9 subjects,
  within-subject, CSP+LDA); the re-run reproduces the paper's macro-F1 values exactly.
* Boundary chart: PAMAP2 PCA-loading baseline (new, `experiments/pamap2/general_pamap2_pca_diag.py`,
  K=7: 0.562 macro-F1) and fresh Opportunity / Indian Pines screens (logs in `data/`).
* Lichens 9-band and Collagen 30-band selections: best configuration at that K in the MasterRun / Normalized results.

## Size check and overlap sweep
The assembled poster was rendered at 90 dpi and inspected row by row for overlaps, clipped text and out-of-box overflow (all clean as of 2026-09-03). Electrode dot maps use an evenly spaced schematic grid so neighbouring circles never touch; the interpolated maps keep true 10-20 coordinates.
`qa_scale_vs_old_poster.png` shows the old IASIM poster and this one at the same physical scale (Method figures and the imaging row) so text and panel sizes can be compared directly. Figures are drawn at their slot size; the placement scale is 0.97–1.11 for every figure (see `build_poster.py`), so figure text is 18–22 pt on the sheet (secondary labels 14–17 pt), captions 20 pt, body 22 pt.

## Text-box sizing rule
PowerPoint applies percentage line spacing to Calibri's full line height (1.22 em), which is taller than LibreOffice's rendering. All text uses 100% spacing and every box is budgeted as lines × size × 1.22 plus margins with a few points to spare, so captions do not clip into the next section bar in PowerPoint.
