# E7 findings: depth-dependent failure of linear unmixing

Settings: uniform cover t = 0.05-1.3 mm (8 values) over the buried NADH/FAD/collagen/elastin layer, noisy fast capture (scale 0.25), NNLS per ROI pixel (central 0.4-0.6, 12 GT pixels from `explain_pixel`). Endmember groups NADH, FAD, matrix (collagen + elastin), scatter (dyes summed over layers). Libraries: thin-cover (t = 0.05 mm) endmembers vs endmembers from the forward model at the capture's own depth, with and without a per-layer split (7 endmembers). Channel sets: joint 8-line EEM, joint 4-line (340-430), 365 nm only. Errors normalised by the true signal at 0.05 mm. Noise-free and noisy data compared. Bootstrap CIs in results.json (rel_bias_ci).

- Detected buried NADH signal falls 10^4-fold from 0.05 to 1.3 mm (e-folding about 0.1 mm); FAD falls 40-fold; cover collagen/elastin ('matrix') and scatter rise 3x. Beyond ~0.4 mm NADH is below 2 % of its shallow value (<<1 % of total signal).
- Joint-EEM NNLS with the thin-cover library returns an NADH abundance 18-32 % of the unburied NADH signal at all depths (true value is <2 % beyond 0.4 mm), i.e. a spurious, nearly depth-independent NADH; relative bias of NADH reaches 10^2 (0.6 mm) to 10^3-10^4 (>= 0.8 mm). FAD error goes from -14 % to ~ +7 %.
- The redox ratio FAD/(FAD+NADH) error grows from -0.12 (0.05 mm) to -0.55 (0.6 mm) and -0.84 (1.3 mm), joint8 thin-library. 365 nm only is not better (error +0.48 -> -0.12 but non-monotone, condition number E 112 vs 5.3 joint-8, up to 2e4 right-depth).
- Fix attempts: (1) endmembers simulated at the right depth does NOT fix it (NADH error 11-126 % of the shallow level; redox error -0.57 at 0.6 mm and -0.95 at 1.3 mm: worse); (2) splitting endmembers per layer (buried vs deeper dye) helps somewhat for the redox ratio at 0.4 mm (-0.07 vs -0.42) and NADH (2-32 %), but the condition number rises to 11-68 and the redox error returns to -0.43...-0.85 for t >= 0.6 mm. (3) The noise-free curves are nearly identical to the noisy ones: the failure is structural (collinearity of NADH, FAD and collagen/elastin emission plus a vanishing NADH signal), not photon noise.
- Reference floor: already at t = 0.05 mm the unmixing error is -33 % to +60 % per endmember, residual fraction 0.14-0.29 even noise-free; capture MC noise (15 % photon fraction), the band-response approximation in endmember electrons and the layer mixture within a dye group explain this. Read depth-induced error as growth relative to that floor.
- Joint 4-line EEM has a worse conditioning than joint 8 (10 vs 5) but a lower residual fraction (0.05-0.11); 8-line vs 4-line abundance errors are similar.

Take-home: linear unmixing of buried NADH/FAD under a 0.4-1.3 mm scattering collagen cover fails because the buried NADH signal vanishes (10^-4) and the cover's own fluorescence dominates; the apparent NADH and the redox ratio are dominated by leakage from cover signatures (redox error -0.5 to -0.9). Depth-matched or layer-resolved endmembers do not restore it, so depth-resolved or physics-based (surrogate) inversion is needed rather than spectral unmixing.
