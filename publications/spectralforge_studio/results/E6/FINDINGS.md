# E6 findings: cover-thickness mapping on wedges

Settings: tile 4 x 4 output px at scale 0.25 (87 x 64 px; 144 tissue / 108 phantom tiles scored, wedge 0.1-1.4 mm tissue, 2-0 mm phantom), free amplitude, uniform-thickness 1-D surrogate (tissue 248 channels; phantom 6 excitations), exposure levels 0.003 / 0.03 / 1 relative to nominal, fits with photon-only (eps = 0) or eps = 2 % weights/SE, bootstrap CIs over tiles. Channel sets: all; k = 8 information-optimal (eps = 2 %, thickness + amplitude); 5 random k = 8 draws. Single noise seed.

- Tissue wedge, all channels, level 1: RMSE 405 um (CI 343-469) with photon-only weights; 262 um (220-316) with eps = 2 % weights; bias +89 / +25 um. Level 0.03: 541 / 570 um; level 0.003: 776 um (bias -675 um).
- Phantom wedge, all channels: RMSE 246 / 201 / 184 um (levels 0.003 / 0.03 / 1; eps = 0), 160 um with eps = 2 % at level 1; bias +33 to +104 um. RMSE saturates at ~160-200 um as exposure increases: accuracy is bias (model) limited, not photon limited.
- Nominal 95 % CI coverage (|err| < 1.96 SE): at level 1 only 8 % (tissue) and 5 % (phantom) with photon-only SE (mean SE 23 / 3 um vs errors 100-400 um); with eps = 2 % SE 19 % / 13 %. Coverage looks acceptable at low exposure (0.88 phantom level 0.003) only because the photon SE (180 um) is large. Photon-limited CIs are badly over-confident, as the CRLB addendum predicts; even eps = 2 % does not restore nominal coverage: the model bias (wedge slope vs uniform-training surrogate, MC noise in surrogate grid, interpolation) exceeds 2 % relative error.
- k = 8 information-optimal channels vs all channels: tissue level 1, 274 um vs 405 um (photon weights) and 274 vs 262 (eps = 2 %); coverage 0.54 vs 0.08. Random k = 8: 498 +/- 91 um (5 draws). Phantom: all channels are better (160-184 um) than optimal k = 8 (247-335 um) and random (527-540 +/- 380 um). The k = 8 sets were optimised on the tissue Fisher information for thickness, not on mapped accuracy, and the phantom set is tied to its own scene; optimality for the CRLB does not guarantee better bias.
- Error vs thickness (tissue, level 1, all, eps = 2 %): RMSE 260 / 261 / 266 um in bins 0-0.5 / 0.5-1 / 1-2.1 mm; phantom 150 / 120 / 199 um.
- Photon-limited tile CRLB (thickness + amplitude) for the same data: tissue 17 um (level 1), 210 um (0.03), 1.8 mm (0.003); phantom 2.7 um (level 1). Observed RMSE exceeds the CRLB by 15-70x at level 1.
- Caveats: quick-mode tissue accuracy is worse; 1-D surrogate trained on uniform-thickness blocks, so lateral transport at the wedge slope is unmodelled; one noise realisation; tiles are not independent (wedge gradient along x).

Take-home: wedge thickness maps are retrievable to ~160-260 um RMSE (10-20 % of the 0.1-1.5 mm range) at nominal exposure, limited by model bias rather than photons; reported photon-based CIs are over-confident by an order of magnitude, and eps-aware weighting improves RMSE 35 % and coverage 2.5x but does not fix it. Channel reduction to 8 information-optimal channels keeps accuracy on the tissue scene but not universally.
