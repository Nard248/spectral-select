# E4: inclusion detectability (fluorescent nodule, absorbing vessel) under a collagen cover

**Settings.** 16x16 mm block: 4 mm dermis_reticular | 7 mm autofluorescent tissue (E2 "buried" optics: NADH 60, FAD 40, collagen 250, elastin 40 uM) | `collagen_dense` cover of thickness d (Jacques-type optics, as E2). The inclusion top touches the cover/tissue interface, so d = depth of the inclusion top = cover thickness. Nodule: sphere r = 0.5/1/2 mm of `dysplasia_nodule` optics with NADH 400 / FAD 150 / collagen 60 uM (6.7x / 3.8x host NADH/FAD). Vessel: cylinder (axis z) r = 0.25/0.5/1 mm of `blood_oxygenated`. Lab TLS lines 340/365/400/430 nm at the protocol exposures (1000/250/50/80 ms), Nuance LCTF (31 bands), native 348x256 pixels = 0.21 mm/px on the block; 3D MC with voxel inclusions (0.1 mm), 1.2e6 photons per pass (preview_fraction = 1), emission node step 40 nm, 3 seeds (0,1,2). Depths: nodule 0.05-3 mm (11 values), vessel 0.05-2 mm (10 values). 141+84 captures (~2.2 h on a loaded 14-core M4 Pro; the 90-min budget was exceeded because the first depth grid, 0.25-3 mm, showed that all structure lies below 0.5 mm and had to be refined). ROI = projected disc (nodule) or strip |x|<=r, |z|<=5 mm (vessel); background = annulus/strips r+0.5...r+2 mm. Signal = band-summed expected electrons over bands >= lambda_ex+25 nm (excludes the first, elastic-tail band; the all-band variant is in `results_all_bands`). Delta = [ROI - annulus]_with inclusion - [ROI - annulus]_matched no-inclusion capture (same seed). 
**Common random numbers** (`per_photon_streams=True`) are not exposed by `block_capture`; they were enabled by monkey-patching `transport3d.excitation_pass/emission_pass` in `E4_scene.py` (no src edit).

**Noise decomposition.**
- Sensor (shot + read + dark, band-summed over 30 bands): sigma_pix = 55 / 49 / 44 / 46 e- per native pixel at 340/365/400/430 nm (background ~2400 / 1300 / 250 / 340 e-). The analytic variance agrees with 40 `sensor.detect` realisations (ratio 1.000).
- Monte Carlo (1.2e6 photons): smooth, ~mm-correlated, ~5-8 % of the signal per pixel. RMS of the ROI-annulus contrast of a no-inclusion capture (pure MC noise of one capture): 340 nm 58-139 e- (nodule rois) / 58-68 (vessel); 365 nm 28-69; 400 nm 5-12; 430 nm 12-36. At 340/365 nm the single-capture contrast is therefore MC-limited (MC > sensor noise); at 400/430 nm it is sensor-limited.
- CRN benefit (nodule r=1, 365 nm; SD of Delta across 3 seeds): d=1 mm: 28 e- (CRN) vs 81 (same-seed plain) vs 106 (independent baseline seed); d=3 mm: 27 vs 41 vs 154. CRN reduces MC noise of Delta 1.5-6x, but the annulus pixel-wise noise of (with - without) stays ~90 e- vs ~130, because the emission-pass source list (hence photon-to-voxel mapping) changes when an inclusion is added. Not a complete cancellation.
- "Instrument CNR" = |mean_seeds Delta| / sigma_pix. "Raw single-capture CNR" = |ROI - annulus|_no baseline / sqrt(sigma_pix^2 + sigma_MC,pix^2). Conservative MC floor of the 3-seed Delta (sqrt2 x null RMS / sqrt3, no CRN credit): 340 nm 48-113 e- (= 0.9-2.1 sigma_pix), 365 nm 23-57, 400 nm 4-10, 430 nm 10-29.

**Findings.**
1. **Vessel (absorbing contrast) is detectable only at shallow depth, mainly in the UV.** Per-pixel instrument CNR = 3 depth (log-linear interpolation; seed-bootstrap 95 % CI, 3 seeds so crude): 340 nm: 0.24 mm (0.18-0.39) for r = 0.25; 0.39 (0.14-0.40) for r = 0.5; 0.44 (0.30-0.50) for r = 1. At 365/400/430 nm per-pixel CNR is already < 3 at the shallowest depth (0.05 mm; peaks 2.5-3.1 at 365 nm, 1.6-1.9 at 430 nm, 0.3-0.5 at 400 nm). Delta is a dip (negative), ~-260 e- at d <= 0.25 (340 nm, r=1) and falls below the MC floor beyond ~0.5-0.75 mm.
2. **ROI-averaged CNR** (noise of the ROI mean over its native pixels; larger inclusions help): CNR = 3 depth for the vessel 340 nm: 0.67 / 1.47 / 1.34 mm; 365 nm: 0.58 / 1.21 / 1.26; 400 nm: <0.05 / 0.82 / 1.15; 430 nm: 0.41 / 0.50 / 0.60 mm (r = 0.25 / 0.5 / 1). CIs are wide (see results.json) and values beyond ~0.75 mm are within ~2x the MC floor: treat as upper bounds.
3. **Fluorescent nodule: not detectable at any depth, wavelength or size** with this protocol and scene (peak per-pixel instrument CNR 2.6 for r=0.5 / 340 nm; no depth reaches 3, and the peaks are of the order of the MC floor, 1.0-2.1). Delta of the nodule is not monotonic and changes sign with depth and seed (MC noise ~ 50-110 e-). The reason is physical, not a bug: with the 1.2 mM-collagen cover, the cover's own autofluorescence (~1e-6 vs ~1e-12 for the tissue/nodule components in `explain_pixel` at 365 nm, d=0.5 mm) is the background, and the excitation is absorbed within the cover (mu_eff(340-365 nm) = 10-15 /mm, 1/mu_eff = 0.07-0.1 mm). The nodule changes the image mostly through optical contrast (its mu_s differs), modulating cover fluorescence.
4. **Decay length vs diffusion theory (vessel).** Fitting |Delta| ~ exp(-d/delta) on the points with d >= 0.15 mm and |Delta| > 2x MC floor (4-6 points, R^2 0.5-0.97), delta_fit = 0.5-1.7 mm (e.g. 340 nm: 0.66 / 0.60 / 1.06 mm for r = 0.25 / 0.5 / 1; 365 nm 0.55 / 0.49 / 0.89; 400 nm 1.41 / 0.95 / 1.38; 430 nm 1.72 for r=1; bootstrap CIs 0.4 to >4 mm). The "buried fluorophore" prediction 1/(mu_eff(ex)+mu_eff(em)) of the cover is 0.07 (340) - 0.5 mm (430): the measured lengths are 5-10x larger. The emission-only length 1/mu_eff(lambda_em) = 2.0-3.2 mm (lambda_em,eff 458-497 nm) is 2-4x larger than the fits. The vessel does NOT see a buried emitter attenuated twice by the cover; it absorbs the diffusely transported emission of the autofluorescent cover/tissue, so contrast decays with the emission-side diffusion length (partly shortened by the finite fit window and the shape of the near-field). No nodule fit is possible (signal inside the MC floor).
5. **Wavelength ordering.** 340 nm > 365 nm > 430 nm > 400 nm in per-pixel CNR despite 340/365 nm having the largest MC noise; 400 nm is limited by the 50 ms protocol exposure (background 250 e-, Delta only 12-24 e-), 430 nm by the elastic-leak dominated signal (the all-band 430 nm CNR is higher: see `results_all_bands`).
6. **The constant ring offset** of W2 cancels with the matched capture: the raw single-capture CNR (no subtraction) is 1.3-3x lower than the instrument CNR at shallow depth.

**Caveats.**
- Only 3 seeds and 1.2e6 photons: MC noise on Delta (50-110 e- at 340/365 nm) is comparable to the signal at d >= 0.5 mm, so "max depth" values from CNR = 3 are upper bounds with crude bootstrap CIs; a 20x photon count would be needed to bring MC below sensor noise at 340 nm. Alternating, depth-correlated wiggles (e.g. vessel d = 0.75 vs 1.0 mm) are shared-baseline MC noise, not physics.
- The nodule composition (NADH 400 / FAD 150) is an assumption (metabolically active nodule), not a literature value; exogenous labels were screened (EBFP2, DAPI, brightener, fluorescein, PpIX at 10-50 uM, d = 0.5-1 mm, r = 1): none gave a positive Delta because of the cover absorption. The result is specific to the dense-collagen cover (E2); a clearer cover would give mm-scale depths.
- Depth = cover thickness (the inclusion top touches the interface); the cover is itself autofluorescent, which is the dominant background.
- Per-pixel CNR depends on the native pixel (25.8 um sensor pixel, 0.21 mm on the block); ROI-averaged CNR depends on the inclusion area. Protocol exposures differ between lines (1000 ... 50 ms), so wavelength comparisons include exposure.
- 0.1 mm voxels: the r = 0.25 mm vessel is only 2.5 voxels in radius.
- Fits use 4-6 points; CIs are wide.

**Take-home.** With the lab protocol and a dense autofluorescent collagen cover, absorbing vessels are detectable per pixel only to ~0.2-0.45 mm (340 nm) and ROI-averaged to ~0.4-1.5 mm; endogenous-contrast fluorescent nodules are not detectable at all because the cover autofluorescence background and the 0.07-0.1 mm UV excitation depth overwhelm them. Contrast decays on the emission-side diffusion scale (~0.5-1.7 mm), not on the two-way excitation+emission scale.
